Vacancies: Project Manager (PSA010 and PSA011) | Deadline: 30 December 2026

Project Managers sought for PSA010 (Social Evaluation) and PSA011 (Emotion Differentiation). Learn more about the projects by reading the proposals in the PSA Project Updates section of this newsletter.

Project Managers will help coordinate timelines, teams, and communication across multiple groups, making this a great opportunity to gain leadership and big team science experience. No prior project management experience is required, and support will be provided. Expected commitment is approximately 2–4 hours per week, though workload may vary during different stages.

See full details on Slack. Applications are open until 30 December or until the positions are filled, so apply quickly!

Contact John Protzko (Protzko@gmail.com), Sergio Barbosa De La Torre (PSA010 lead author; sergio.barbosad@urosario.edu.co), or Claire Whiting (PSA011 lead author; claire.whiting@princeton.edu).

Opportunities for PSA Members

Call for Collaborators: ManyCouples

Register collaboration interest with ManyCouples, a large-scale dyadic study on love and well-being in romantic relationships. Join over 150 scholars from 60 countries who have already signed up.

As a collaborator, you must collect data from at least 30 couples via a personal online link (students, panels, and your own networks are all fine), and in return, you will be invited to collaborate on resulting papers. See full details.

Call for Papers: Zeitschrift für Psychologie | Deadline: 15 February 2027

Submit abstract to Zeitschrift für Psychologie (published exclusively in English), for an issue on “Systematic Meta-Research for Examining and Understanding Effect Heterogeneity”.

This issue focuses on advancing our understanding of why research findings vary across studies. Of particular interest are submissions that systematically investigate how different characteristics in study design, materials, measures, sample of participants, specific settings, timing, or contextual conditions effect heterogeneity.

Call for Papers: Work, Aging and Retirement | Deadline: 14 April 2027

Submit proposal to Work, Aging and Retirement, for an issue on “The Dynamics of P-E fit: Implications for Career Sustainability Across Career Stages”.

The issue focuses on the development, disruption, and maintenance of person-environment (P-E) fit across career stages and their implications for career sustainability.
